How every dish
comes to life

From the fields of Saran to your table — care at every single step.

Step 01

Source

Fresh vegetables, sattu, milk and spices come every morning from local farmers and markets around Chapra.

Step 02

Prepare

Masalas are ground in-house and sattu filling is mixed by hand with ajwain, garlic and mustard oil.

Step 03

Cook

Littis are roasted on coal, and handi is slow-cooked on a low flame so every bite keeps the real desi flavour.

Step 04

Serve

Served hot at your table with a smile, in kulhad and steel thali. No rush, sit as long as you like.
